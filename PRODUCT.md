# Project Financial Freedom

## Goal

Project Financial Freedom is a personal finance app designed to provide a clear and honest picture of my financial situation and how it changes over time.

It is intended for monthly financial reviews rather than daily bank-account monitoring.

## The Problem

Managing money can feel confusing when information is spread across bank accounts, credit cards, loans, assets, and statements.

The app should bring this information together and answer:

- What is my net worth?
- How much did I earn and spend?
- Where did my money go?
- Am I following my budget?
- Is my financial situation improving?
- What requires my attention?

## Monthly Workflow

Once a month, I will:

1. Collect statements from my accounts and credit cards.
2. Prepare standardized CSV files, optionally using an external AI assistant.
3. Upload the files to the app.
4. Review unknown transactions, duplicates, and transfers.
5. Update manually tracked assets and liabilities.
6. Confirm that the information is complete.
7. Close the month and review the resulting report.

A month remains provisional until all relevant statements provide coverage through the end of that month.

## Financial Reports

Each completed month should provide:

- Net worth and its change.
- Total income.
- Total expenses.
- Savings and savings rate.
- Budget progress.
- Debt movement.
- Spending by category.
- Important changes from previous months.
- Recurring expenses and subscriptions.
- Missing, uncertain, or outdated information.

Every number should be understandable and traceable to its source.

## Statements and Dates

Statements do not need to follow calendar months.

For example, if a credit card closes on the 15th, transactions from one calendar month may appear across two statements. The app should combine them according to when the transactions occurred.

The previous month can be closed once every account has supplied enough information to cover its final day.

## AI Assistance

AI may be used outside the app to convert statements into the required CSV format.

AI can help:

- Extract transactions.
- Suggest categories.
- Normalize merchant names.
- Identify possible transfers or duplicates.
- Highlight uncertain information.

AI must not invent missing financial information. Unknown values should remain unknown until reviewed.

The app is responsible for validating, reconciling, storing, and reporting the final information.

## Product Principles

### Trust over automation

Financial information must be correct, explainable, and reviewable.

### Simple monthly routine

The app should make completing a monthly review easy without requiring daily attention.

### Decisions over dashboards

Reports should help me understand what happened and decide what to do next.

### No double-counting

Transfers and credit-card payments should not appear as additional income or expenses.

### Honest uncertainty

Incomplete months, stale asset values, and unknown transactions should be clearly identified.

### Progress without judgment

The app should encourage better financial decisions without using shame or overwhelming me.

## Initial Scope

The first version should focus on:

- Accounts and credit cards.
- CSV transaction uploads.
- Monthly income and expense tracking.
- Transaction categorization.
- Transfer and duplicate detection.
- Basic budgets.
- Manual assets and liabilities.
- Net-worth history.
- Monthly closing and reporting.

Daily bank synchronization and built-in AI integrations are not required initially.

## Definition of Success

The app succeeds if, after completing a monthly review, I can confidently explain:

- What I own.
- What I owe.
- What I earned.
- What I spent.
- What changed.
- Whether I am moving in the right direction.
- What action I should take next.
